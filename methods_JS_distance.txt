Spatial ligand-receptor co-localization by Jensen-Shannon distance. For each CellChatDB ligand-receptor pair, the spatial distribution of ligand expression (all cells) and receptor expression (within each CD4 T cell subset: Th0, Th1, Th2, Th17, Treg) were computed as 2D sum-per-bin histograms on a shared 50 um grid, Gaussian-smoothed (sigma = 1 bin), and normalized to probability vectors. Jensen-Shannon (JS) distance (base 2) between each ligand-receptor pair quantifies spatial co-localization, where 0 indicates perfect overlap and 1 indicates non-overlapping distributions. Multi-subunit receptor complexes were expanded to individual subunit pairs. Pairs were retained only if the receptor was detected in at least 10% of cells in at least one subset. Differential co-localization between subsets was summarized as delta JS = JS_A - JS_B, with positive values indicating greater co-localization in subset B. This analysis was applied to three pairwise comparisons (Th17 vs. Th0, Treg vs. Th0, Th2 vs. Th0) in the acute HDM day 3 sample.
